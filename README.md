## Origin

Great projects have small beginnings. Vue was first written in an apartment. Linux began with an email about "just a hobby". The spotlight always arrives late; the story always starts in a corner.

We live in an age defined by star projects. Charts, trends and corporate endorsements decide which code deserves to be seen. History keeps proving otherwise: the projects that change the world usually begin in ignored repositories. OpenFork exists so that these repositories are no longer buried.

---

## Philosophy

> Great projects are not born great. They begin with a single commit, a single issue, a late-night experiment.

Our mission has two halves that lock together. The first half is to find underestimated small projects outside the spotlight, and to turn the light toward them through search, curation and discovery. The second half is to lower the entry point of participation: a fork, a star, an issue, all count. Great projects are not waited for; they are carried up by ordinary people, one contribution at a time. When enough people carry, small projects become great ones.

---

## Three Beliefs

> **One** · See the small. That is how you see what is coming.

> **Two** · Every fork is a vote. Every contribution is an incubation.

> **Three** · Open source is great not because it belongs to giants, but because it belongs to everyone.

---

## Principles

| Principle | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Anchor on the small | Do not chase popularity; chase potential. Taste is earned by noticing what others skip. |
| Action before applause | One contribution outweighs a hundred shares. |
| Decentralized | Do not depend on giants or single capital. Greatness survives outside the spotlight. |
| Long-term | We tend the garden; we do not harvest it early. |
| Accessible to all | Low barriers, open participation, easy to replicate. Open source is a common right, not a privilege. |

---

## How to Participate

1. **Fork an underrated repository**: bring it into your sight first.
2. **Star the projects you care about**: let both algorithms and people see them.
3. **Answer an issue, fix a bug, improve a document**: every contribution counts.
4. **Share the treasure repositories you find**: recommendation is the best spotlight.

---

## Vision

> When small projects no longer need to "grow up" before being seen,
> when everyone can participate easily and create together on equal footing,
> the people's era of open source will truly arrive.

OpenFork does not invent trends. It only makes sure that, before the tide arrives, the repositories worth seeing are seen.
